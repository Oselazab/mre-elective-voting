# Elective Registration: What the 2020 Bylaws Say

This guide summarizes the elective structure and prerequisites listed in the *Mechatronics & Robotics Engineering* 2020 bylaws. It describes the academic plan in that document; it is not a current registration-office notice.

## Practical registration sequence supported by the document

The bylaws establish the study-plan slots and list prerequisites, but they do **not** describe a student-facing registration procedure. Based on what the PDF does specify, a student preparing to register should:

1. Identify the elective slot in the study plan for their level and term.
2. Choose a course from the bylaw elective list that is being offered for that term.
3. Check the course's listed prerequisite(s) against completed coursework and ask the department to confirm eligibility or equivalency if needed.
4. Complete registration through the university's current official registration process and follow its dates, capacity rules, and approval requirements.

The PDF does not name a registration portal, dates, seat limits, add/drop process, override procedure, GPA rule, or method for assigning students to electives. Those details must come from current faculty or registrar instructions, not from this summary.

## How the voting website relates to registration

The website is a **preference poll**, not official course registration. Its current configuration asks a student to select six open courses from 26 and keeps CSE 426 and EEP 456 visible as closed because they were already selected for this poll. This site setting does not alter the bylaw's seven elective slots, confirm prerequisites, guarantee that a course will run, or enroll a student.

Before treating poll results as final registration choices, the program should reconcile the poll's six-choice configuration and the two closed courses with the seven elective slots in the 2020 plan and confirm the current offering and prerequisites.
